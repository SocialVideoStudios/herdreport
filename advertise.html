Questions

Who reads the ad?

Scott reads every spot himself, in his own words, from the talking points you provide. Host-read ads sound like part of the show, which is why listeners trust them.

What's the difference between pre-roll, mid-roll and post-roll?

Pre-roll plays at the start of the episode, mid-roll in the middle of the conversation and post-roll at the end. Each episode has a limited number of slots, and the booking calendar only shows dates that are still open.

How far ahead should I book a Spotlight Series episode?

Give Scott as much lead time as you can before your sale so the episode can be recorded, edited and released with time for buyers to watch. Add your sale date when you request it.

How do I pay?

Once Scott approves your booking, you'll receive an invoice. Online card payment is coming soon.

Can I change or cancel a request?

You can cancel any request that's still pending from your client account. For changes after approval, contact Scott directly.
