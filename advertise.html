Questions

Do I need an account to look around?

No. Browse and fill your cart first. You'll create an account at checkout so Scott can reach you and you can track your bookings.

Who reads the ad?

Scott reads every spot himself, in his own words, from the talking points you provide. Host-read ads sound like part of the show, which is why listeners trust them.

What are the five spots?

Pre-roll plays at the start of the episode. Mid-roll 1, 2 and 3 are breaks during the conversation. Post-roll closes the episode. Each spot is sold to one advertiser per episode, so open weeks go first come, first served.

How do I pay?

Add the spots you want to your cart and pay at checkout. That's it.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes. Cancelled bookings are refunded, minus a $50 cancellation fee. To cancel or make changes, contact Scott.
